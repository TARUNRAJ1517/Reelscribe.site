POLICY

How your data is handled.

What we collect, why, and how to control it.

Information We Collect

We store your email address to manage your account and sign-in. Uploaded videos are processed temporarily and deleted immediately after a transcript is generated.

How We Use Your Data

Your email is used only to manage your account, plan, and usage limits. We never sell your data to third parties.

Transcripts & Clips

Generated transcripts are linked to your account and saved in your history. Generated clips are stored temporarily and automatically deleted.

Cookies

We use only the cookies necessary to keep you signed in.

Data Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. Uploaded videos are deleted immediately after processing.
