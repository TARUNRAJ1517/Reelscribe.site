—FAQ
Do I need to keep the tab open while it processes?

Yes for now — keep this tab open until the clips finish rendering.

What happens to my clips after 24 hours?

Clips are automatically deleted from our servers 24 hours after they're generated. Download anything you want to keep before then.

Can I change captions after generating?

Not yet — caption style is baked into the clip during rendering. Set your style before hitting Generate.

Why did I get fewer than 12 clips?

The AI only cuts moments it scores as genuinely strong — a shorter or less eventful source video may yield fewer clips.
